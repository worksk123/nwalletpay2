How to install

Install in four steps

N Wallet is installed directly from this page, so Android asks for one extra permission the first time.

1

Download

Tap Download for Android. The file nwallet.apk saves to your phone.

2

Open the file

Open it from the notification bar or your Downloads folder.

3

Allow & install

If asked, allow installs from your browser in Settings, go back and tap Install.

4

Open the app

Tap Open, create your account and read the in-app terms.

Your phone may show a "this type of file can harm your device" message. Android shows it for every app downloaded outside the Play Store. Only continue if you trust the source.

FAQ

Common questions

Is N Wallet free?

Yes. Downloading and installing the app from this page is free.

I downloaded the file. What next?

Open nwallet.apk from your notifications or Downloads folder, tap Install, then tap Open. Your tasks appear after you open the app and create an account.

Can I earn a fixed amount every day?

No. Rewards, where available, depend on the tasks you complete and the app's terms, and they can change. N Wallet should not be treated as a source of income.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

N Wallet is offered as a direct download from this page. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser the first time. This is standard for direct downloads.

Which phones are supported?

Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer. The app is not available for iPhone.

How is my data handled?

The app collects only what it needs to run your account. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
